# Month 1 summary

##Question 
Which ward settlements in Eti-Osa LGA are more than 2 km from a health facility?

##Operation
Buffered GRID3 health facilities by 2 km, intersected, then took the difference against ward settlements and calculated the percentage of settlements outside the 2km of health facilities.

##Expected
Two or three peripheral wards flagged

##Got
Six wards with less than 30 percent of settlements outside the health facilities 2km buffer.
Ijeh/Dolphin Estate, Ajiran/osapa, Sangotedo, and Okun Ajah community are the wards with settlements outside the 2km buffer.

## What surprised me
The settlements that fall outside the 2km buffer analysis are very few.
231 out of 5537 fall outside

## Limitations, stated plainly
- straight-line distance, not travel distance. A settlement within 2km of a health facility is not actually 2km 

## What I still need
- population per ward, to convert area uncovered into people uncovered, which is the number that actually matters
